About PocketWorkshop

Simple browser-based tools for everyday tasks.

What is PocketWorkshop?

PocketWorkshop is a growing collection of free online tools designed to make small everyday tasks easier. The goal is simple: useful tools, clear interfaces, and as little hassle as possible.

What kind of tools are available?

PocketWorkshop includes tools for text, images, PDFs, and other common tasks. Current tools include Word Counter, Image Resizer, Image Compressor, PDF Merge, and PDF Split.

Why use browser-based tools?

Many simple tasks should not require installing software, creating an account, or uploading files to a remote server. PocketWorkshop focuses on tools that can run directly in your browser whenever practical.

Privacy-first approach

Current text, image, and PDF tools are designed to process your content locally in your browser. Your selected files and text are not intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop servers for processing.

No account required

PocketWorkshop currently does not require registration or a user account. Open a tool, use it, and download the result when needed.

Still growing

PocketWorkshop is an ongoing project. New tools and improvements may be added over time based on usefulness, reliability, and how well they fit the idea of quick everyday utilities.
