PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

PocketWorkshop is designed to keep your files and text on your device whenever practical.

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Overview

PocketWorkshop provides free browser-based utility tools. We aim to collect as little information as possible and to process user content locally in the browser whenever practical.

2. Files and text you use with our tools

Current PocketWorkshop tools are designed to process text, images, and PDF files locally in your browser. Files or text you select for these tools are not intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop servers for processing.

This includes tools such as Word Counter, Image Resizer, Image Compressor, PDF Merge, and PDF Split.

3. Cloudflare Web Analytics

PocketWorkshop uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand general website usage and performance, such as page views, referring websites, device type, browser, country, and page performance.

Cloudflare states that Web Analytics is privacy-first, does not use cookies or localStorage for usage measurement, and does not collect or use visitors' personal data for Web Analytics.

4. Website hosting and security

PocketWorkshop is hosted using Cloudflare services. Cloudflare may process technical information necessary to deliver, secure, and operate the website in accordance with Cloudflare's own privacy policies and service terms.

5. Third-party libraries

Some tools may load open-source JavaScript libraries from third-party content delivery networks (CDNs), such as unpkg. These providers may receive ordinary technical request information when your browser loads those library files. Your selected documents or images are not intentionally sent to those CDN providers by PocketWorkshop.

6. Cookies

PocketWorkshop currently does not use advertising cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics itself does not use cookies for analytics measurement. Cloudflare infrastructure or security features may use strictly necessary technologies where required to provide or protect the service.

7. Advertising and affiliate links

PocketWorkshop currently does not display third-party advertising. If advertising, affiliate links, or additional tracking technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain those services and any choices available to visitors.

8. External links

PocketWorkshop may link to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when PocketWorkshop adds new tools, analytics, advertising, or other services. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.

PocketWorkshop is a simple utility website and does not currently require user accounts.
