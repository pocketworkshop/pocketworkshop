ABOUT THIS TOOL

Count text instantly.

Word Counter gives you a quick overview of your text without requiring an account or uploading anything.

What it counts

See words, characters, characters without spaces, sentences, paragraphs, and an estimated reading time.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste your text into the box above.
  2. The counters update automatically as you edit.
  3. Use Copy text or Clear when you are finished.

Common uses

Useful for essays, articles, emails, product descriptions, social posts, application forms, and any task with a length limit.

Privacy

Your text is processed locally in your browser and is not intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop for counting.
