ABOUT THIS TOOL

Handle the most common percentage calculations in one place.

Useful for shopping discounts, growth rates, price changes, schoolwork, budgets, statistics and everyday comparisons.

Percentage of a number

Use the first calculator when you know the percent and the original number, such as 20% of 75.

Percentage share

Use the second calculator to find what percentage one number represents of another, such as 30 out of 120.

Increase or decrease

Use percentage change to compare an old value with a new one and see how much it rose or fell.

Privacy

All calculations happen locally in your browser. No values are intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop.
