ABOUT THIS TOOL

Convert Unix time in either direction.

Useful when logs, APIs, databases or code use numeric timestamps instead of human-readable dates.

Seconds and milliseconds

Unix timestamps commonly use seconds, while JavaScript and many APIs use milliseconds. This tool supports both.

UTC and local time

See the same moment as UTC, ISO 8601 and in your browser's local time zone.

Date conversion

Enter a date and time and choose whether to interpret it as your local time or as UTC before converting.

Privacy

All conversions happen locally in your browser and are not intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop.
