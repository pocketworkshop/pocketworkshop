ABOUT THIS TOOL

Create clean slugs for URLs and filenames.

Convert human-readable titles into compact strings that are easier to use in page URLs, blog posts, docs and simple filenames.

What is a slug?

A slug is the readable part of a URL, such as easy-home-office-ideas in /blog/easy-home-office-ideas.

Useful options

Choose hyphens or underscores, remove accents, convert to lowercase, replace ampersands and optionally limit the final length.

Length limiting

When a maximum length is set, the tool avoids leaving a trailing separator and tries to stop at a clean word boundary.

Privacy

Your text is processed locally in your browser and is not intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop.
