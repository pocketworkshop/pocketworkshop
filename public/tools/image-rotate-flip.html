ABOUT THIS TOOL

Fix image orientation or mirror an image quickly.

Rotate by quarter turns, flip horizontally or vertically, and save the result without uploading the image.

Rotate

Turn an image 90° left, 90° right or 180°. Width and height are swapped automatically for quarter turns.

Flip

Mirror the image horizontally or vertically. Rotate and flip operations can be combined.

Save format

Keep the original format or save as JPG, PNG or WebP. JPG and WebP quality can be adjusted.
