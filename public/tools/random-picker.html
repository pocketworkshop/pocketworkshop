ABOUT THIS TOOL

Pick fairly from a list without a spreadsheet.

Useful for names, teams, chores, giveaway entries, lunch ideas, activities and any other list of choices.

One item per line

Paste a list from a spreadsheet, document or message. Each line becomes one choice.

One or many picks

Choose one winner or several. By default, an item cannot win twice in the same draw.

Randomness

The picker uses the browser's cryptographic random-number generator when it is available.

Privacy

Your list and results stay in your browser and are not intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop.
