ABOUT THIS TOOL

See what changed between two text versions.

Useful for drafts, configuration files, copied notes, code snippets and other line-based text comparisons.

Line alignment

The checker aligns matching lines so an inserted or deleted line does not automatically make every following line look different.

Comparison options

Ignore capitalization, trim surrounding whitespace, or treat repeated spaces as a single space when matching lines.

Color cues

Removed lines are highlighted on the original side, while added lines are highlighted on the changed side.

Privacy

Both texts are compared locally in your browser and are not intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop.
