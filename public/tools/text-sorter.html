ABOUT THIS TOOL

Put lists and text lines in the order you need.

Useful for names, filenames, IDs, numbers, copied lists and any other one-item-per-line text.

Multiple sort modes

Choose alphabetical, natural, numeric, line-length or random ordering without installing anything.

Natural sorting

Natural order understands embedded numbers, so item2 can appear before item10 instead of being sorted as plain text.

Original text preserved

Case and surrounding whitespace can be ignored for comparison without rewriting the original content of each line.

Privacy

Your text is sorted locally in your browser and is not intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop.
