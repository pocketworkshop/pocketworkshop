ABOUT THIS TOOL

Put PDF pages in the order you want.

See page previews, move pages up or down, remove unwanted pages, and download a newly organized PDF.

How to use it

  1. Select a PDF file.
  2. Use Up and Down to rearrange pages.
  3. Remove any pages you do not need.
  4. Create and download the organized PDF.

Page previews

Small page previews make it easier to identify pages before you move or remove them.

Common uses

Useful for fixing scanned documents, rearranging forms, removing blank pages, or preparing a cleaner PDF before sharing it.

Privacy

The organizing process runs locally in your browser. Your selected PDF is not intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop for processing.
