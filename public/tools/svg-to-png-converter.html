ABOUT THIS TOOL

Turn scalable SVG artwork into a PNG image.

Useful when a website, app, document or social platform needs a raster PNG instead of an SVG file.

Choose your output size

Set width and height manually, use the SVG's original dimensions, or multiply the result with 2×, 3× or 4× scale.

Transparency

Keep transparent areas transparent, or turn transparency off and choose a solid background color for the PNG.

SVG compatibility

The converter uses your browser's SVG renderer. External fonts, linked images and unsupported SVG features may render differently.

Privacy

Your SVG and PNG are processed locally in your browser and are not intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop.
