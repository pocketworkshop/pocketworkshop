ABOUT THIS TOOL

Add a simple text watermark before sharing an image.

Useful for drafts, previews, portfolio images, social posts and other images where you want to show ownership or status.

Position

Choose one of nine common positions from top-left to bottom-right, including centered placement.

Appearance

Adjust text size, opacity, color, rotation and edge margin until the watermark is visible without overpowering the image.

Output

Save the finished image as PNG, JPG or WebP. JPG and WebP include a quality control for file-size tuning.

Privacy

Your image is processed locally in your browser and is not intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop.
