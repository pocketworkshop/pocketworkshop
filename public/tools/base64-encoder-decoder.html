ABOUT THIS TOOL

Encode or decode Base64 without losing Unicode text.

The converter uses UTF-8, so non-English text and emoji can be encoded and decoded correctly.

Standard Base64

Use the regular Base64 alphabet for general text encoding and decoding.

URL-safe Base64

Use the URL-safe variant when Base64 needs to fit more cleanly into URLs, filenames or tokens.

Unicode support

Japanese text, accented characters and emoji are handled as UTF-8 rather than being limited to plain ASCII.

Privacy

Your text is processed locally in your browser and is not intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop.
