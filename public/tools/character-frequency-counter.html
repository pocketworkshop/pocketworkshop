ABOUT THIS TOOL

Find the most common characters or words.

Useful for writing analysis, puzzle making, language study, data cleanup and quick text inspection.

Character mode

Counts visible characters and symbols. Whitespace can be included or ignored, and modern browsers keep many emoji sequences together as a single grapheme.

Word mode

Counts words using browser language segmentation when available, with a Unicode-aware fallback for letters, numbers and apostrophes.

Sort and filter

Sort by frequency or alphabetically, then filter the table to quickly locate a specific character or word.

Privacy

Your text is analyzed locally in your browser and is not intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop.
