ABOUT THIS TOOL

Resize images without installing software.

Change image dimensions and export JPG, PNG, or WebP files directly in your browser.

What it does

Set a new width or height, keep the original aspect ratio when needed, choose an output format, and control quality for supported formats.

How to use it

  1. Choose a JPG, PNG, or WebP image.
  2. Enter the width or height you want.
  3. Choose the output format and quality.
  4. Resize the image and download the result.

Common uses

Handy for website uploads, profile images, documents, email attachments, online forms, and images that need exact pixel dimensions.

Privacy

Your selected image is processed locally in your browser and is not intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop for resizing.
