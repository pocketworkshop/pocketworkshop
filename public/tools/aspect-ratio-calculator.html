ABOUT THIS TOOL

Keep images and video dimensions proportional.

Useful when resizing images, videos, thumbnails, social posts, presentations and website graphics without stretching them.

Simplify dimensions

1920 × 1080 simplifies to 16:9, while 1200 × 1200 simplifies to 1:1.

Calculate a missing side

Enter a new width or height and the calculator finds the matching dimension using the current ratio.

Common presets

Quickly load common formats such as 1:1, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9, 9:16, 4:5 and 21:9.

Privacy

All calculations happen locally in your browser. No files or dimensions are intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop.
