ABOUT THIS TOOL

Measure the gap between two calendar dates.

Useful for deadlines, project schedules, travel planning, contract periods, countdowns and everyday date calculations.

Total days

Shows the exact whole-day difference between the selected dates. Inclusive mode adds both endpoints to the count.

Calendar difference

Also shows the human-readable gap in calendar years, months and days, such as 1 year, 2 months and 5 days.

Weekdays

Weekday totals count Monday through Friday only. Public holidays are not removed.

Privacy

All calculations happen locally in your browser. Your selected dates are not intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop.
