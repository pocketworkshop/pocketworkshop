ABOUT THIS TOOL

Escape text for HTML—or turn entities back into text.

Useful when HTML-sensitive characters such as angle brackets, ampersands and quotes need to be stored or displayed safely as text.

Encode

Characters such as <, > and & are converted to HTML entities so browsers treat them as text.

Decode

Named and numeric HTML entities can be converted back to their readable characters.

Unicode option

Turn on numeric entities when you want non-ASCII characters, including Japanese text and symbols, represented as character references.

Privacy

Your text is processed locally in your browser and is not intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop.
