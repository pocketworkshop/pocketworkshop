ABOUT THIS TOOL

Clean repeated entries without rearranging your list.

Useful for email lists, IDs, keywords, URLs, copied spreadsheet values, logs and any other line-based text.

Preserves order

Unique lines stay in their original order. You can choose whether the first or last duplicate occurrence should survive.

Flexible matching

Choose whether capitalization and surrounding whitespace should matter when identifying duplicate lines.

Original text stays intact

Matching options affect comparison only. The kept line retains the original spelling, capitalization and spacing you entered.

Privacy

Your text is processed locally in your browser and is not intentionally uploaded to PocketWorkshop for duplicate removal.
